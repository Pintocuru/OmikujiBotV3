## What is this template? (Features)

### A generator that gives OneComme BOT features

- "OmikujiBot" is a generator that adds BOT features to OneComme.
- When someone posts a specific word (such as "おみくじ") in chat, a random result is shown on your stream screen.
- It can also trigger under specific conditions, such as for first-time viewers (their first-ever comment) or a viewer's 100th comment overall, and display something on your stream screen.
- The BOT character is "Strawberry Chocolat", an original character of the Pintoku project (Pin to Kuru Kikaku).
  - She has a caring, big-sister vibe, and her sweet, polite hospitality is her charm.

### Strawberry Chocolat's Character Profile

![Illustration of Strawberry Chocolat (smiling)](../../images/StrawberryChocolate-1792723579455.webp)

- **Personality**
  - A caring, attentive type who can't leave someone in trouble alone.
  - She has a big sister's calm and warmth, and puts people at ease.
- **Manner of speaking**
  - Polite and soft-spoken as a rule.
  - She speaks in a tone that gently wraps people up, and occasionally adds humor to lighten the mood.
  - She addresses people with a friendly "-san", with a slightly indulgent nuance.
    - If you misbehave, she might tease you with "-chan"?
